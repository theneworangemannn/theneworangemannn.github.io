ourday Photo Drop

Private wedding photo delivery for the couple and their guests.

What this app does

Guests can send photos from a wedding invitation page. The photos are accepted privately and delivered to a Google Drive folder controlled by the couple. Guest uploads are not presented as a public gallery.

Google Drive access

ourday Photo Drop uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission is used only for files and folders that the app creates or that the user explicitly makes available to the app. The app does not request broad access to unrelated files in the user's Google Drive.
