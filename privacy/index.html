Privacy Policy

ourday Photo Drop

ourday Photo Drop is a private wedding photo delivery service. This policy explains how information is handled when guests send photos and when the couple connects Google Drive for delivery.

Information handled

The service may handle photos submitted by guests, an optional sender name, technical delivery records needed to process an upload, and Google OAuth credentials required to deliver photos to the couple's Google Drive.

How Google user data is used

The service requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. Google user data is used only to create or manage the destination folder and files used by this app for private photo delivery. The service does not use this access to browse unrelated files in the user's Drive.

Sharing

Guest photos are intended for the couple and are not displayed as a public guest gallery. Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Uploaded photos and delivery records may be stored on private service infrastructure as needed to receive, deliver, retry, and operate the service. Files delivered to Google Drive are retained according to the Drive account owner's own choices and settings.

Security

OAuth secrets and refresh tokens are intended to be stored as private server-side configuration and are not intentionally exposed to guests or public pages.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The current version will remain available at this URL.
